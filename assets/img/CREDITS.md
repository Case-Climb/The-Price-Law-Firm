## Two important notes

**These are stock photographs, not the firm.** None of them show Bill Price,
his office, his staff, or the actual courthouses named on the location pages.
The Tallahassee, Pensacola and Destin images do show those real places; the
Panama City and Marianna images are representative Gulf Coast and highway
scenes, not those specific towns. Replace them with genuine local and firm
photography when it becomes available — real photos of the actual office and
attorney outperform stock on a local service site.

**`bill-price.jpg` is not stock** — it is the client's own photograph of Bill
Price in front of the firm's signage, supplied directly and used in both
headshot slots (`/` and `/about`). No stock photo of an unrelated person was
ever used for him.

Note its source resolution is only **600×599px**, which upscales and softens on
wide or high-DPI screens. Request the original full-resolution file and replace
it at the same path — no code change is needed.

To re-download or add images, see `scripts/fetch-image.sh` and
`scripts/images.txt`.
